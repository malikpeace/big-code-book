BIG CODE BOOK

From "a terminal is magic" to reading, supervising, and changing Memento. Six weeks. One hour a day. No memorizing.

HOW THIS WORKS

One chapter a sitting, about an hour. Each one teaches one idea with a picture and a story, then shows you the same idea inside Memento's real code, then makes you do one small thing with your hands. Nothing here can touch the real Memento. The toy app you break is called Memento Jr.

Words are defined the first time they appear. Tap any dotted word to see what it means at the level you have reached. Definitions get deeper as you go.

Tags on every section: NEED you need this to run Memento. ENGINEER a junior should know it. RABBIT HOLE later, if ever, collapsed by default.

ON A PHONE?

Reading and the in-page code boxes work here. Anything that needs Terminal shows a "Mac only" card. Skip it and come back on the Mac. Your progress is saved on each device separately.
